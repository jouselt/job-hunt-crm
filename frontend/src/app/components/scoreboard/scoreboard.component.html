Scoreboard

Every offer in your CRM and every live vacancy from the devsChile feed, ranked by one score computed against your profile. One ruler for both, so the numbers compare. The big number is relative to the best in your list (100 is your top match); the small one is the raw ranker points, the same value the Python scorer produces.
